Min and max size of a deck is 6 cards
5 of them you must own and 1 must be from another user's account (for the application this can probably be ignored)

Every card has to be unique

you cannot use two cards that are associated with the same character
ie if card A is associated with characters A and B and card B is associated with character B then only one of those cards can exist in the deck

the character that the deck is intended for can also not exist in the deck
ie if the deck is for character A I cannot use any card associated with character A in the deck
